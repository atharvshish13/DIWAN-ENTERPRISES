Our Story

Who We Are

Diwan Enterprises was founded on a simple belief: every brand deserves high-impact, verifiable advertising that actually reaches its target audience. With 14 years of experience in sales and marketing of agricultural inputs and 7 years working in this profession, we bring unmatched field knowledge to every campaign.

Headquartered in Amravati, Maharashtra, we operate active outlets in Akola, Nagpur, Jalna, Nashik and Sangli — with project coverage extending to Indore (MP), Raipur (CG), Ahmedabad (GJ), Karnataka and Telangana.

  • 45+ enterprise clients including TVS, Bayer, Syngenta, UPL, Corteva, IFFCO, Amul, Mahindra & more
  • GPS-verified van campaign delivery with 4 daily location photos and odometer readings
  • In-house fabrication for L-Shape, Hut-Shape & T-Shape customized promotional vehicles
  • Full-stack event capabilities: stages, AV, anchors, branding, catering & logistics
